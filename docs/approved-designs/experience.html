Tell us about yourself. Where and what are you studying?

I'm in the third year of a B.Sc. in Life Sciences at St. Xavier's College, Mumbai. I like fieldwork more than bench work, which is why I applied to ecology labs.

How did you apply, and what were the stipend, duration and place?

Through the Indian Academy of Sciences Summer Research Fellowship. Applications opened in November and results came out in the last week of February. You pick guides from the Academy's list and write a short statement on why their work interests you.

I was placed with a plant ecology group at the Centre for Ecological Sciences, IISc, for eight weeks, with a stipend of ₹12,500 a month and a room in the fellows' residence.

How competitive was the selection, and what helped you get in?

Very. Seniors told me only a small share of applicants get a place. What helped was a statement that named a specific question from my guide's recent paper, and a semester project I could talk about in detail. Marks matter, but they were not the whole story.

What work did you do during the internship?

I measured how grazing changes plant growth in grassland plots, then ran the statistics in R. The first two weeks were reading and learning the lab's methods. After that I had my own bench space and a weekly meeting with my guide, who let me test my own idea in the last month.

What was good about the experience, and what was hard?

Good: the lab, the people and the campus library. The other fellows became close friends. Hard: the experiments failed more often than they worked, and Bengaluru in June was wetter than I expected for fieldwork. Budget for an umbrella and for autos to the field station.

What are your key takeaways?

I learned to plan an experiment so that a failed run still teaches something, and to write up results every evening instead of at the end. It confirmed that I want to do a master's in ecology.

Advice for students applying

Start your statement in December, not the night before. Read two recent papers from each guide you choose and say what you would like to try. If you don't get in the first time, apply again: two fellows in my batch were selected on their second attempt.
