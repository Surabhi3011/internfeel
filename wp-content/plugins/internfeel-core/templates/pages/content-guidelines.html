InternFeel works because every experience is honest and first-hand. Before you submit, check yours against these guidelines. We review every experience within 24 hours.

What you can share

  • Any academic experience lasting from 3 days to 2 years: internships, training, industry or field visits, workshops and apprenticeships.
  • Only your own experience. Write in your own words.

Write so others can use it

  • Be specific: how you applied, how selection worked, what you did, the stipend and the hours.
  • Be fair. Criticism is welcome when it is about the experience, not personal attacks.
  • Give advice you wish you had been given.

What we don't publish

  • Experiences you didn't have, or that were written for you, paid for, or copied from elsewhere.
  • Names, phone numbers, emails or other personal details of supervisors, colleagues or other students.
  • Confidential information you agreed not to share.
  • Abuse, harassment, hate, or claims you can't stand behind.
  • Advertising, or links to programmes that charge students a fee.

Posting anonymously

Anonymous posts hide your name, photo and LinkedIn. Your college, course, year and the details of the internship stay visible, so write knowing that people close to you may still recognise it.

Proof and the Verified badge

Upload an offer letter, completion certificate or similar document that shows your name and the organisation. Only you and InternFeel admins can open it. A matching document earns the Verified badge and, once the experience is published, three invites.

Comments

The same rules apply to comments. Ask questions, add your own tips, and be kind. You can edit each comment once. Anyone can report a comment or an experience; three reports hide it until an admin decides.
