InternFeel shows ads in a few labelled slots. These rules keep them from getting in the way of honest reviews.

  • Every ad is labelled Advertisement or Sponsored, and a page carries at most three.
  • Paying for ads never affects which experiences are published, verified, ranked or moderated.
  • We do not advertise internships or programmes that charge students a fee.
  • An organisation's own ads never appear on reviews of that organisation.
  • Students who are under 18 see only non-personalised ads.
  • Ads load only after a visitor agrees to advertising cookies.

Sponsored placements for organisations open after the relaunch. To ask about them, email contact@internfeel.com.
