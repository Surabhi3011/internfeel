InternFeel ("we") runs internfeel.com, where students read and share first-hand accounts of internships. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), or your Google sign-in; your full name, display name, college, course, graduation year, whether you are under 18, and, if you add it, your LinkedIn profile.
  • What you write: experiences, ratings, advice and comments, and drafts you have not yet submitted.
  • Proof of an internship: files you upload so we can verify an experience. They are stored privately and only you and InternFeel admins can open them.
  • Invites: who invited you, the invite links you make, and a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address used to make them, kept only to spot misuse.
  • Reports and moderation: what you report and our decisions, kept in a moderation log.
  • Cookies and similar technology: a sign-in cookie, a cookie that remembers an invite for 30 days, your cookie choices, and, only if you agree, advertising and analytics cookies from Google.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, publish and moderate experiences, verify proof and issue certificates.
  • To credit the student who invited you, and to stop invite and report abuse.
  • To show dashboard notifications. We do not send notification emails; the only email we send is the password-reset link you ask for.
  • To show ads and measure visits, only after you consent.

What others can see

Published experiences show your display name, college, course and graduation year. If you post anonymously, your name, photo and LinkedIn are hidden everywhere on the site; the other details stay visible. Your certificate shows your full name, and its public verify page shows the name but not the title of an anonymous experience. Proof files are never public.

Cookies, ads and analytics

Google AdSense and Google Analytics load only after you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time from Cookie settings in the footer. Students who tell us they are under 18 see only non-personalised ads.

Students under 18

Students under 18 may join. We will ask for verifiable parental consent before the Act's rules on children's data apply on 13 May 2027.
