InternFeel began in 2012, when Arjit was still in college and had just finished his first internship. He realised how little students knew about an internship before they applied, so he built InternFeel from the ground up. With a small team, he began collecting first-hand experiences from students across the country.

Years later, Arjit and Surabhi are bringing InternFeel back. The aim is the same, with more room for students to share their experiences and help each other. Peep before you leap.

Written by students, for students

Every experience here is written by the student who did the internship. Each one answers the same questions: how they applied, how hard it was to get in, what the work involved, what they were paid, and what they’d tell a junior. That makes it easy to compare a research fellowship at an IIT, or an NGO field project with a role abroad.

Reviewed, verified and certified

We read every experience before it goes live, usually within 24 hours. You can post under your name or anonymously. Add proof, such as an offer letter, and you get a Verified badge. Everyone whose experience is published gets a certificate from InternFeel.

Add yours

More than 200 students have already shared their stories here. If you’ve done an internship, research programme or training, add yours. It takes about 20 minutes and could save someone else months of guessing.
