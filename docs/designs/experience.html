How did you find and get the internship?

I saw the fellowship on my department notice board in January. The application asked for a one-page proposal and a short video. I was shortlisted in March and had two interviews: one technical, one about why I wanted to do research.

What did you work on?

A low-cost network of soil-moisture sensors for small farms. I designed the sensor board, wrote the firmware and tested battery life in the field. By the end, twelve nodes were reporting every fifteen minutes.

What was a typical day like?

A stand-up at 9:30, then bench work until lunch. Afternoons were for firmware and reading papers. Twice a week we drove out to the test farm, which was the best part.

How was the mentorship?

My mentor met me every Monday and replied to messages quickly. When I was stuck on power consumption for a week, she paired with me for an afternoon and we found the bug together.

What did you learn?

PCB design, how to read a datasheet properly, and how to plan an experiment so the data actually answers the question. I also learnt to say early when something isn't working.

Anything you'd change?

Housing was not provided and the stipend only just covered rent near the lab. Start looking for accommodation as soon as you get the offer.

Advice for future applicants

Make the proposal specific. Mine named the exact sensor and the problem with existing ones, and that is what the interviewers asked about.
