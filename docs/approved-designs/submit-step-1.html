Before you start

  • Keep your offer letter or certificate handy. You can add it as proof in step 4, or later from your dashboard.
  • Write the way you'd tell a junior: dates, money, the tools you used.
  • Posting anonymously hides only your name, photo and LinkedIn. Your college, course and the details stay visible.
  • We review every experience within 24 hours. Your certificate is ready as soon as it's published.

The questions in step 2

  1. Tell us about yourself. Where and what are you studying?
  2. How did you apply, and what were the stipend, duration and place?
  3. How competitive was the selection, and what helped you get in?
  4. What work did you do during the internship?
  5. What was good about the experience, and what was hard?
  6. What are your key takeaways?
  7. Has it changed what you want to do next?
