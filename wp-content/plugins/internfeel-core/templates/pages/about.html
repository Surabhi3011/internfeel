InternFeel is where students share what internships are really like: how they applied, how selection worked, what the work was, the stipend, and the advice they wish they'd had. Peep before you leap.

How it works

  • Read first-hand experiences of research, industry, government, NGO and virtual internships.
  • Share your own in about 20 minutes. We review every experience within 24 hours.
  • Get certified: every published experience earns a certificate of contribution that anyone can verify.
